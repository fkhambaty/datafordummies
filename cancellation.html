Cancel a subscription

Pro renews until you cancel it. Cancellation stops the next renewal. It does not delete a payment Razorpay has already captured.

  1. Sign in and open your account.
  2. Choose “Cancel at end of billing cycle”.
  3. The Worker asks Razorpay to cancel at the end of the current cycle.

Until that date, Pro lessons stay open. After it, the account returns to the free lessons. The demo account cannot cancel a subscription because it is never charged.

If the button fails, email fktiindia@gmail.com from the address on the account and include the plan shown on your account page. Do not send a card number.
